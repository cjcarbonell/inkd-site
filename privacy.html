inkd Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

inkd is a fountain pen and ink log made by Carbonell & Co. It is built to keep your collection private. This page explains what the app does with your information, in plain language.

The short version

  • We do not collect your data. Carbonell & Co never receives your pens, inks, photos, or notes.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • Your collection lives on your device, with an optional backup in your own iCloud.

What inkd stores

Everything you enter, such as pens, inks, nibs, purchase details, notes, ratings, photos, and the dates you inked and cleaned a pen, is stored on your device in the app's own storage.

iCloud backup

inkd can back up your collection to your private iCloud storage using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your own iCloud account and is covered by your Apple account's protections. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. You can turn iCloud off for inkd in your device's Settings.

Camera and photo library

inkd asks for access to your camera and photo library only when you choose to add a pen photo or scan an ink label. Photos you add are stored with your collection. Reading a label to fill in ink details happens on your device and the image is not sent to us or to any other service.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, inkd schedules reminders on your device to tell you when a pen may need cleaning. These reminders are created and delivered locally by your device. No information leaves your phone for this.

The home screen widget

The inkd widget shows which pens need cleaning and your collection counts. It reads a small summary saved on your device and shares it only between the app and its widget.

Exporting and importing

If you export your collection to a file, or import one, that file goes wherever you choose to save or share it. inkd does not send it anywhere.

Third parties

inkd does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and does not share your information with anyone. Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and usage reports if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings.

Children

inkd is a general-audience hobby app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
